# Rejuran Clinic Research — Seoul, One-Day Trip

Research compiled 2026-05-16 for a 38-year-old male planning a single full-face
Rejuran ("salmon DNA") session in Seoul on a one-day visit.

## TL;DR

- **Your instinct is right.** Korean beauty-app reviews (UNNI / Gangnam Unni) are
  incentivized and skew positive. Do not rank clinics by star score or review count.
- **One session is a "glow," not a transformation.** Rejuran is a 3-session protocol.
  A single session gives subtle hydration/elasticity that mostly fades by ~week 9.
  Worth doing as a one-day treat; don't expect lasting structural change.
- **A truly objective "top 10" ranking does not exist** — there is no neutral dataset,
  the apps are pay-to-play, and "best clinic" blogs are lead-generation. What follows
  is a vetting *framework* plus a ranked read on the three clinics you screenshotted.
- **The VAT refund for foreigners ended 1 Jan 2026** — budget for full local price.
- **Verify the actual Rejuran box** (DOT mark + QR/NFC scan) at the clinic.

## 1. Are app reviews trustworthy? (Short answer: no, not as a ranking)

UNNI / Gangnam Unni is the best of the Korean beauty platforms, but:

- Clinics **pay for visibility**; promoted listings sit next to organic ones.
- Reviews are **incentivized** — patients get discounts/credit for posting reviews and
  photos, so the pool is self-selected toward happy customers who wanted the discount.
- Korean patients with bad outcomes typically **stay silent** (face-saving culture,
  fear of defamation suits — Korea has criminal defamation even for true statements).
- Scores cluster at 9.0+, making them nearly useless for ranking. A 9.1 vs 9.9 tells
  you almost nothing.
- Reported issue: repeated reviews — same users, same photos, multiple 5-star posts.

**How to actually use the app:** ignore the headline score. Instead read the
**lowest** reviews first, look for **specific detail** (consultant name, the sting of
the injection, day-by-day bruising, real-lighting photos), and treat **generic glowing
text as noise**. The independent Korean community **Sungyesa** (성예사) is considered
the most trustworthy non-incentivized source, though it is Korean-language.

## 2. What actually matters when picking a Rejuran clinic

1. **Doctor does the injection, not a nurse/consultant.** In high-volume clinics a
   consultant sells and a nurse injects. For Rejuran technique matters (depth, spacing,
   bruising). Confirm a licensed doctor performs it.
2. **Genuine product.** Ask to see the Rejuran box. Authentic PharmaResearch product
   has a **DOT mark** and a **QR code / NFC tag**; scanning confirms authenticity.
   Counterfeit and grey-market Rejuran is a known problem. Product must be kept frozen.
3. **Specialist, not a side menu.** A clinic where Rejuran is a core treatment (high
   Rejuran volume, ultrasound-guided anesthesia, dedicated protocol) beats one where
   it's an add-on to surgery or lifting.
4. **"Factory" vs "premium" tell.** Factory clinics: consultant talks more than the
   doctor, aggressive upsell, prices that look too cheap, assembly-line pacing.
   Premium clinics: doctor-led consult, private rooms, slower pace. Both can be fine —
   know which you're walking into.
5. **English support confirmed in writing** before you go.
6. **Transparent all-inclusive price** (consultation, numbing, needles, aftercare).
   Ballpark full-face Rejuran Healer in Gangnam: **₩200,000–400,000 (~$150–300)**.
7. **No upsell pressure.** Decline same-day add-ons (laser, extra boosters). One day,
   one treatment, leave.

## 3. The three clinics you screenshotted

All three are Gangnam Station-area, English-supported, and visible on UNNI. App scores
are deliberately discounted below.

### MOCCI Clinic — best fit for your goal
- 9.5 / 961 reviews. Top 3 procedures are **all Rejuran variants** (Healer 520,
  HB Plus 191, Eye 76) — a genuine Rejuran specialist.
- Ultrasound-guided injection anesthesia, private treatment rooms, premium positioning.
- Open Sunday 11:00–16:30 (so likely open Monday — confirm).
- Caveat: newer clinic (opened Dec 2024), smaller track record/volume.

### Seoul i Plastic Surgery Clinic — highest volume, factory-style
- 9.1 / 6,967 reviews; Rejuran Healer #1 with **1,835** — enormous injector experience.
- It's a **plastic surgery** clinic running Rejuran as a high-throughput offering:
  expect efficient, possibly assembly-line service and upsell. Closed Sunday.
- Good if you want cheap + fast + a very practiced needle; weaker on personal attention.

### TheiA Clinic — lifting-focused, Rejuran is secondary
- 9.9 / 2,465 reviews, but their identity is **lifting** (Ultherapy/Titanium); Rejuran
  is only #4 (439). Open Sunday 10:00–21:00 — best hours.
- The 9.9 score is suspiciously high; treat it as a marketing artifact.
- Fine clinic, but not the Rejuran specialist of the three.

## 4. Ranked recommendation for your one day

Confidence is **moderate** — based on positioning, specialization and the vetting
framework above, not on verified outcome data (which no one has).

1. **MOCCI Clinic** — Rejuran specialist, premium, ultrasound anesthesia, good Sunday/
   Monday hours. Best match for "one careful session, treated well."
2. **Seoul i Plastic Surgery** — pick if budget-driven or you value a very high-volume
   injector and don't mind factory pacing. Confirm a doctor injects.
3. **TheiA Clinic** — solid backup, best hours, but least Rejuran-focused.

To extend toward a "top 10," apply Section 2 to any clinic — but note that brand names
on "best Rejuran clinic" blogs are mostly SEO/lead-gen and not independently verified.

## 5. Practical plan for Monday

- **Tonight:** use the app's "Chat & Book with Clinic" to (a) confirm Monday hours and
  a slot, (b) confirm English, (c) get the all-inclusive price in writing, (d) confirm
  a doctor performs the injection, (e) confirm genuine Rejuran Healer for full face.
- **Bring** your passport.
- **Time budget:** ~30–45 min numbing + ~15–30 min injection ≈ 1–1.5 hr in-clinic.
- **At the clinic:** ask to see the Rejuran box; check DOT mark and scan the QR/NFC.
- **Downtime:** pinpoint bumps/redness for 12–24 h; possible small bruises 3–7 days
  (concealable with makeup from day 1). Flying home is fine — bruising is only cosmetic.
- **Avoid alcohol** the night before and 24 h after (worsens bruising). Skip same-day
  add-on upsells.
- **Expectations:** subtle glow/hydration peaking weeks 1–4, fading by ~week 9. For
  real results you'd need to repeat 2–3× — not possible on a one-day trip.
