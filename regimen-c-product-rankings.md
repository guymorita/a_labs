# Regimen C — Deep Research: Optimal Dosages & Product Rankings

For each active in Regimen C: the clinically effective target, plus ranked product
picks split by region — **US / Global** and **Asia** (Korea + Japan).

Pricing is approximate (Spring 2026, USD). Where the same brand sells globally
(COSRX, Anua, Medicube, etc.) the cheaper/genuine source in Asia is noted.

## 1. Retinoid (face) — the headline active

**Clinical target.** Tretinoin 0.025–0.05% (Rx) is the gold standard. OTC: retinal
(retinaldehyde) **0.05–0.1%** ≈ retinol **0.5–1%**. Adapalene 0.1% (Differin OTC) is
true Rx-strength and FDA-approved. Encapsulated/time-release formulas reduce
irritation without losing efficacy.
